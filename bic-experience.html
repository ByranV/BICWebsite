A college, not a conventional school

BIC offers a different approach to the traditional schooling experience. We combine the academic rigour of the Cambridge curriculum with a supportive, college-style learning environment that recognises the potential of every individual.

Education here is about who students become, as well as what they achieve.

school

College-style learning

A comfortable, college-style environment that students want to be part of, with a relaxed dress code at our Bryanston and Pretoria campuses.

trending_up

A more mature environment

As students progress, their timetable becomes increasingly flexible, giving them more control over their time on campus as they mature into responsible young adults.

groups

Small, supportive communities

Small class sizes create supportive learning communities in which every student is known.

person

Individual academic attention

Teachers work closely with each student, with academic guidance at every level, from subject choice to final Cambridge exams.

task_alt

Responsibility and independence

Students are challenged to think independently and take responsibility for their learning, with the guidance and support they need to succeed.

Mentors

Skills for university and life

Alongside their Cambridge qualifications, BIC students develop the skills to navigate an increasingly complex world:

  • Critical thinking
  • Independent thinking
  • Communication
  • Leadership
  • Research and problem-solving
  • Self-management
  • Personal responsibility
  • Confidence
  • Resilience

A college community

Campus life

Students have level-specific areas where they can spend time with their peers between classes, a Student Recreation (“Rec”) Room with a canteen, and basketball and badminton courts in our gardens.

Student Liaison Council (SLC)

Each year the student body is led by our Student Liaison Council, which helps plan student events throughout the year, both for each level and for the whole college.

Excursions and camps

Cultural, academic and social excursions, events and year-group camps encourage students to try new things and broaden their interests.

Arts, enrichment and leadership

Our students shine beyond the classroom too. In 2026 Refilwe Molatlhegi represented South Africa at the Dance Fest Novi Sad International Competition in Serbia, winning three first places.
