At BIC, we integrate a wide range of digital learning tools and eResources into our courses to support student achievement and ensure flexible access to education.

Our eResources provide students with instant access to:

  • High-quality study materials
  • Interactive learning platforms
  • Online academic and administrative support

These digital platforms allow students to engage with their education asynchronously at a time that suits them. Making these eResources available to our students, we promote independent learning, support diverse learning styles, and enhance overall academic performance.

Our commitment to innovation in education helps students:

  • Deepen their understanding of subjects
  • Stay organised and on track
  • Achieve their full academic potential in a digital-first world

Online Student Services (OSS)

OSS is British International Colleges’ dedicated online support platform, designed to enhance the educational journey of our students. Offering seamless access to study materials, exam entry guidance, and administrative support, OSS empowers learners to stay organised and succeed academically. With user-friendly features and a commitment to excellence, it bridges the gap between students and their goals in an increasingly digital academic world.

For more details, visit OSS.

MyiMaths

MyiMaths is an interactive online platform designed to make learning maths engaging and effective. It provides a wealth of resources, including interactive lessons, homework tasks, and assessment tools, all aligned with various international curricula. Teachers can easily track student progress, while students enjoy a fun and interactive approach to mastering mathematical concepts.

Explore more at MyiMaths.

Mind Joy

Mindjoy is a platform dedicated to transforming education through the integration of AI-powered tools and interactive learning solutions. It offers innovative features, bringing science, technology, engineering, and mathematics to life through engaging visualisations, real-time data, and hands-on experiences. Additionally, its Tutor Insights feature provides personalised feedback for students.

For more details, visit Mindjoy.
