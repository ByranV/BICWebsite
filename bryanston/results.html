Top Achievers in the May/June 2026 Cambridge Examinations

We would like to extend our sincere congratulations to all our students who completed the May/June 2026 Cambridge examinations. Your results reflect the hard work, perseverance and commitment demonstrated throughout the academic year.

Every achievement represents dedication, personal growth and the determination to reach your goals. We are proud of the effort and responsibility shown by each student during their preparation and examinations. Whether achieving outstanding results or making significant personal progress, every student has reason to be proud of their accomplishments.

We would also like to recognise those students whose exceptional results placed them among the top achievers in their respective examination levels. Your achievements are a wonderful reflection of your commitment to academic excellence and serve as an inspiration to your fellow students.

Congratulations to all our students. We wish you continued success in your future academic endeavours!

Message from the Exams Officer

On behalf of the Exams Office and the entire exams team, I would like to extend my sincere congratulations and appreciation to all our students who sat their Cambridge examinations during the May/June 2026 session. Your commitment, cooperation and mature approach throughout the examination period contributed to a smooth and successful session. Cambridge examinations require considerable preparation, discipline and resilience, and we were pleased to see the way you rose to these challenges.

Regardless of your individual results, you should be proud of the effort you invested and the progress you have made. We wish you every success as you continue your academic journey.

Stuart Barnard – Exams Officer
