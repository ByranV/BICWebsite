Why BIC Bryanston?

Our flagship campus offers the full Cambridge journey, from Junior High Year 7 to A Levels, for South African and international students who want a high-quality, globally benchmarked education. We combine the academic rigour of the Cambridge curriculum with a secure, modern, college-style environment that recognises the potential of every individual.

campaign

2027 intake

Join BIC at any stage in 2027: Year 7 to A Levels

BIC introduced a Junior High Year 7 class in 2026. Bryanston now offers the complete Cambridge journey, and for 2027 we have places available all the way from Junior High through IGCSE to AS and A Levels. Junior High builds strong foundations in core subjects, while every stage develops independent learning habits, confidence and critical thinking.

Places available at:
  • Junior High (Years 7–9)
  • IGCSE
  • AS & A Levels
