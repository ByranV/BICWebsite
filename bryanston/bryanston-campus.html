Junior High Year 7 places available for 2027

BIC introduced a Junior High Year 7 class in 2026, so students can begin their Cambridge high school journey from the very start and progress seamlessly through to A Levels. Junior High builds strong foundations in core subjects while students develop independent learning habits, confidence and critical thinking.

A limited number of places remain for 2027.

Why BIC Bryanston?

Our flagship campus offers the full Cambridge journey, from Junior High Year 7 to A Levels, for South African and international students who want a high-quality, globally benchmarked education. We combine the academic rigour of the Cambridge curriculum with a secure, modern, college-style environment that recognises the potential of every individual.
