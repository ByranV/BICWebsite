Study from anywhere, at your own pace

British International Distance College (BIDC) offers a flexible, complete Cambridge education for students who need an alternative to the traditional classroom. Our distance learning courses cover Foundation (Checkpoint), IGCSE and AS Level, so learners across South Africa and beyond can study from any location, in their own time.

BIDC does not offer full-time classes. Instead, every course combines printed course material, digital resources, online support and access to accredited Cambridge exam centres, so our distance students receive the same high-quality education and academic guidance as every college in the British International group.

Who is BIDC for?

flight

Students who travel

Families who relocate or travel often can keep studying the same Cambridge courses wherever they are.

directions_run

Athletes and performers

Students who train or compete in sport, dance, music or the arts can fit their studies around their commitments.

schedule

Students who need flexibility

Learners who need a flexible schedule can work as quickly or as methodically as they wish.

self_improvement

Independent learners

Students who don’t need a full-time classroom and want to take charge of their own learning.
