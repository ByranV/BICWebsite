Courses that put you in control

Our courses prepare students for further study and future careers in a changing world, whether they want to specialise or build wider expertise. Each course is a complete guide through the Cambridge pathway, studied at the student’s own pace.

  1. 1FoundationYears 7–9Comparable to Grades 7 to 9, leading to Cambridge Checkpoint.
  2. 2IGCSEGrades 10–11Core and Extended courses aligned to the Cambridge IGCSE syllabuses.
  3. 3AS LevelGrade 12Comparable to the matric year and suitable for most South African universities.
  4. 4A LevelFull-time campusContinue to the full A Level at our Bryanston or Pretoria campus.

Foundation

Years 7–9

A strong Cambridge foundation for secondary education, comparable to South African Grades 7 to 9. Foundation courses develop skills and confident content knowledge, introduce students to the Cambridge standard early and prepare them for the Cambridge Checkpoint examinations.

Courses include

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Combined Sciences (Biology, Chemistry, Physics)
  • Second languages (local and foreign)
  • Computing skills
  • Humanities and subject integration

trending_upIndependence: students learn to plan and pace their own work, with support from tutors and their Student Advisor.

IGCSE 1 (Core) & 2 (Extended)

Grades 10–11

Courses at both Core and Extended level, aligned to the Cambridge IGCSE syllabuses and comparable to South African Grades 10 and 11. A select number of courses run over two years, giving students time to prepare for their Cambridge exams.

Courses include

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Combined Science
  • Coordinated Science
  • Sciences
  • Second languages (local and foreign)
  • Commerce
  • Arts
  • Languages and humanities

trending_upIndependence: students take more responsibility for their study plan and exam preparation.

AS Level

Grade 12

The twelfth year of education, comparable to South Africa’s Grade 12 (matric) year. Our AS Level courses cover the Cambridge AS Level syllabus while keeping the flexibility of independent study.

Courses include

  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Pure Sciences
  • Commerce
  • Arts
  • Languages and humanities

trending_upIndependence: students study with the kind of independence they will need at university.

Continue to A Level

Full-time campus

BIDC courses go up to AS Level. Students who want to complete the full A Level can continue full-time at our Bryanston or Pretoria campus, staying on the same Cambridge pathway.

What every course includes

  • All required course material, provided upfront in print and online
  • Academic support from tutors throughout the academic year
  • Guidance from our subject specialists on request
  • Student Advisor consultations until you complete your studies
  • Access to Online Student Services (OSS)
