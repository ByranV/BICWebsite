How to register

Registering at BIDC is as easy as 1-2-3.

  1. 1Create your accountRegister as a new parent or guardian on the BIDC registration website and add your child as a student.
  2. 2Choose your coursesSelect the courses you would like to complete. We recommend a free consultation with a Student Advisor first.
  3. 3Complete your purchaseThat’s it! As soon as your course material arrives, you can get started.
open_in_new Visit the Online Registration Portal
person_add

New students

Register as a new parent or guardian, add your child and select their courses. New students also need the documents listed below.

autorenew

Returning students

Parents can register for further courses on the same registration website. Returning students keep their BIC Student Number and Cambridge Candidate Number.

Documents required for registration

  • South African ID or passport of at least two parents/guardians
  • South African ID or passport of the person responsible for the account (if not a parent/guardian)
  • South African ID, passport or birth certificate of the prospective student
  • Latest school report
  • Code of Good Conduct signed by both student and parent/guardian*

* For new students only.

help

Experiencing some issues? Download our Application Form, complete it digitally in a PDF reader and email it to admin@bidc.co.za. A BIDC Student Advisor will contact you and guide you through the final steps of your registration online.
