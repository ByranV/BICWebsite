Exam Entries for BIDC Registered Students

Enter for Cambridge exams when you are ready, with guidance from your Student Advisor.

BIDC students can enter for Cambridge exams, and for our Distance College Exams at specific levels, when they are ready.

As a registered BIDC student you pay only the fees generated for your Cambridge exam: your registration covers the administration fees for writing at any of our Cambridge exam centres. We offer entries for the May/June and October/November series, subject to availability.

Entry deadlines

  • 31 JanuaryMay/June exam series
  • 31 MayOctober/November exam series
info

Entries submitted after these deadlines may still be accepted, but incur an additional penalty fee. Please submit on time to avoid extra charges.

How to enter

  1. Discuss your readiness with your BIDC Student Advisor (recommended before entering for Cambridge or BIDC exams).
  2. Generate a free quote for your chosen subjects and enter for Cambridge exams from your OSS portal.
  3. Write your exams at one of our accredited Cambridge exam centres.
  4. Not writing Cambridge exams yet? You may enter for Distance College Exams instead (October only).
