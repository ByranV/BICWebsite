At British International College (BIC), we integrate a wide range of digital learning tools and eResources into our academic programme to support student achievement and ensure flexible access to education.

Our eResources provide students with instant access to:

  • High-quality study materials
  • Interactive learning platforms
  • Online academic and administrative support

These digital platforms allow students to engage with their education anytime, anywhere, whether they are on campus or studying remotely. By embedding eLearning tools into our curriculum, we promote independent learning, support diverse learning styles, and enhance overall academic performance.

Our commitment to innovation in education helps students:

  • Deepen their understanding of subjects
  • Stay organised and on track
  • Achieve their full academic potential in a digital-first world

Online Student Services (OSS)

OSS is British International College’s dedicated online support platform, designed to enhance the educational journey of our students. Offering seamless access to study materials, exam entry guidance, and administrative support, OSS empowers learners to stay organised and succeed academically. With user-friendly features and a commitment to excellence, it bridges the gap between students and their goals in an increasingly digital academic world.

For more details, visit OSS.

MyiMaths

MyiMaths is an interactive online platform designed to make learning maths engaging and effective. It provides a wealth of resources, including interactive lessons, homework tasks, and assessment tools, all aligned with various international curricula. Teachers can easily track student progress, while students enjoy a fun and interactive approach to mastering mathematical concepts.

Explore more at MyiMaths.

Mind Joy

Mindjoy is a platform dedicated to transforming education through the integration of AI-powered tools and interactive learning solutions. It offers innovative features such as STEM Mode, which brings science, technology, engineering, and mathematics to life through engaging visualisations, real-time data, and hands-on experiences. Additionally, its Tutor Insights feature provides personalised feedback for students and actionable analytics for educators, fostering self-directed learning and informed lesson planning.

Mindjoy’s mission is to empower educators and students by combining cutting-edge technology with tailored educational support, making learning dynamic, relevant, and enjoyable. From advanced computing in classrooms to enriching humanities subjects, Mindjoy provides tools that cater to a variety of learning needs, ensuring that education is both accessible and forward-thinking.

For more details, visit Mindjoy.
