A legacy of academic excellence, powered by Cambridge

At British International Colleges, we believe in nurturing tomorrow’s global thinkers, innovators, and changemakers. With institutions spanning across our campuses, our unified mission is clear: to deliver world-class Cambridge education that empowers students at every stage of their academic journey.

Stage 7 (Year 7) Places Available for 2027

British International College introduced a Stage 7 (Year 7) class in 2026, offering students the opportunity to begin the Cambridge high school journey from the very start and progress seamlessly through to A Levels.

This programme builds strong academic foundations in core subjects. Students develop independent learning habits, grow in confidence, and strengthen critical thinking skills that support long-term success.

A limited number of places remain available for 2027.

Parents are encouraged to secure a place soon to avoid disappointment.

Why choose us?

  • Cambridge-Certified Curriculum: Internationally recognized programs designed to foster curiosity, critical thinking, and academic rigor.
  • Seamless Academic Pathways: From Year 7 to pre-university, each institution builds upon the last to create a coherent, high-quality learning experience.
  • Qualified & Passionate Faculty: Our educators are not just teachers — they’re mentors, scholars, and global citizens.
  • Modern Learning Environments: State-of-the-art campuses and resources tailored to support 21st-century education.
  • Global Community, Local Impact: We shape learners who thrive across borders and make meaningful change where they are.
