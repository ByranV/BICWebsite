Who you become matters as much as what you achieve

British International College is a specialist, academically driven Cambridge college, not a conventional school. From Year 7 to A Levels, in Bryanston, in Pretoria or through our Distance College, we develop capable, independent and confident young people, preparing them for university and life beyond school.

One Cambridge pathway, from Year 7 to university

Since 1993, British International College has offered an exclusively Cambridge International education. Each stage builds on the last, giving students a clear path to internationally recognised qualifications accepted by universities in South Africa, the UK, USA, Canada, Europe and Australia.

  1. 1CheckpointYears 7–9Strong foundations in English, Mathematics and Science.
  2. 2IGCSEAges 15–17A globally recognised qualification, comparable to Grades 10 and 11.
  3. 3AS LevelAges 16+The first half of the A Level, comparable to the Grade 12 (matric) year.
  4. 4A LevelAges 17+Widely accepted for university entrance in South Africa and around the world.
  5. 5University & beyondYour futurePathways to universities in South Africa and abroad, and the skills to succeed there.
