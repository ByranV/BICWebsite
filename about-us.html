About British International College

Founded in 1993, British International College aimed to create a learning environment for the brightest and the best, using the most prestigious internationally recognised curriculum.

Originally, BIC was a sixth-form College offering only A Levels. After great academic innovation, with the evidence of its A Level Students achieve fantastic results, BIC expanded to offer O Levels and IGCSE. The expansion to include the lower secondary courses completed the high school experience with classes for Junior High students beginning their journey through secondary education.

We aim to work with our students in supporting them becoming the brightest and the best that they can be. Our academically focused daily programme helps students perform well in the classroom, while our personalised lifestyle programmes supports students become excellent function adults in a fast-progressing society. Students have a more relaxed dress code which helps them remain comfortable while on Campus.

BIC offers excursions and events that are culturally, academically, and socially supported. Here, our students are free to engage and interact outside school campus and be exposed to new experiences. Some of our Levels have planned camps to further encourage students to try something new and expand their interests.

Our beautiful Campus is located in Bryanston, close to the N1 highway, making it easily accessible to students from all areas in the Johannesburg region. We are also just down the road from a Gautrain bus stop so students commuting from afar have various transport options.

BIC is a registered Cambridge Exam Centre (ZA057). Our students are able to write all their exams, both internal and Cambridge, right here on Campus.

What is the Cambridge Curriculum?

The Cambridge International curriculum is a globally recognised education system developed by Cambridge Assessment International Education, part of the University of Cambridge.

It offers a flexible, skills-based approach to learning that prepares students for success at university and beyond. The curriculum is taught in over 160 countries and is valued for its academic rigour, international benchmarks, and emphasis on independent thinking.

Each stage builds on the one before, returning to previously learnt content and going more in depth. This gives students a clear and progressive academic structure. Cambridge qualifications are accepted by universities in South Africa and around the world, including the UK, USA, Canada, Europe, and Australia.

What we offer

At British International College, we offer the full Cambridge pathway, making it easy to complete high school with an international qualification:

  • Cambridge Checkpoint (Junior High)
  • Cambridge IGCSE (International General Certificate of Secondary Education)
  • Cambridge AS & A Levels (Advanced Level)

BIC is Cambridge Associate, offering an exclusively Cambridge education to our students. Our college-style approach to learning provides a comfortable environment that students want to come to learn.

On Campus, we have a team of experienced administration staff who are ready to help students throughout their high school career. Academic guidance is provided at all levels support students in curating their own pathways and choosing their subjects for their future careers.

We have highly qualified subject specialists trained in the Cambridge way. Learning is encouraged both in the lectures on campus and in practical ways forming an amalgamation of experiences which promotes real learning and application.

Our students gain attributes to essential real-life due to curriculum integration and academic expectations on students to complete their qualifications successfully.
