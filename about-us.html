Our story

British International College is a specialist Cambridge college, founded in 1993. Whether they study at our Bryanston or Pretoria campus or through our Distance College, our students leave with more than excellent results: they become independent thinkers, confident communicators and capable young adults, ready for university and life beyond school.

British International College (BIC) was founded in 1993 with a clear aim: to create a learning environment where ambitious students achieve their best, built on the most respected internationally recognised curriculum.

BIC began as a sixth-form college offering only A Levels. As our A Level students went on to achieve outstanding results, we expanded to offer IGCSE and Cambridge Checkpoint, so that students can now complete their entire secondary education with us.

In 2001 we launched British International Distance College, bringing the same Cambridge courses to independent learners anywhere in South Africa and beyond. Today BIC teaches full-time in Bryanston and Pretoria, and both campuses and the Distance College are registered Cambridge exam centres.

Our purpose, values and approach

Our purpose

Our mission

To empower every student to achieve academic excellence, develop independent thinking and discover their individual potential.

Our vision

To develop young people who leave BIC not only with internationally recognised qualifications, but with the confidence, character and capabilities to make a success of their future.

What we believe

We believe that education should do more than prepare students for examinations. It should equip them with the knowledge, skills, confidence and independence to succeed in an increasingly complex world.

We combine the academic rigour of the Cambridge curriculum with a supportive, college-style learning environment that recognises the potential of every individual. We challenge students to think independently, take responsibility for their learning and strive for excellence, while providing the guidance and support they need to succeed.

What is the Cambridge curriculum?

The Cambridge International curriculum is developed by Cambridge International Education, part of the University of Cambridge. It is taught in more than 160 countries and is valued for its academic rigour, international benchmarks and emphasis on independent thinking.

Each stage builds on the one before, revisiting earlier content in greater depth and giving students a clear, progressive structure. Cambridge qualifications are accepted by universities in South Africa and around the world, including the UK, USA, Canada, Europe and Australia. As a Cambridge Associate, BIC offers an exclusively Cambridge education across the full pathway:

  1. 1CheckpointYears 7–9Strong foundations in English, Mathematics and Science.
  2. 2IGCSEAges 15–17A globally recognised qualification, comparable to Grades 10 and 11.
  3. 3AS LevelAges 16+The first half of the A Level, comparable to the Grade 12 (matric) year.
  4. 4A LevelAges 17+Widely accepted for university entrance in South Africa and around the world.
