Excellence you can see

At BIC, academic excellence is demonstrated every year: in our students’ Cambridge results, in the awards they earn and in the universities they go on to attend.

It rests on the academic rigour of the Cambridge curriculum, taught by subject specialists in a supportive, college-style environment that recognises the potential of every individual.

Results at a glance

After every Cambridge examination session, we recognise the students whose results placed them among the top achievers at their level. In the two most recent sessions, 28 BIC students earned top-achiever recognition across Checkpoint, IGCSE, AS and A Level.

Highlights

  • At AS and A Level, one Bryanston student was a top achiever in five subjects: Biology, Chemistry, English Language, Mathematics and Physics.
  • At IGCSE, one Bryanston student was a top achiever in eight subjects, from Biology, Chemistry and Physics to Economics, History, Literature in English and Sociology.
  • A Distance College student was a top achiever in five IGCSE subjects: Biology, Business Studies, First Language English, Foreign Language French and Mathematics.
  • Three Distance College students were Checkpoint top achievers, two of them in all three core subjects: English, Mathematics and Science.
  • Top results span the sciences, mathematics, business, accounting and economics, the humanities, art and design, and languages including Afrikaans, French and Mandarin Chinese.
