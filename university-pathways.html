From Year 7 to university and beyond

Every stage of the Cambridge pathway at BIC is designed with the next step in mind. Students leave with internationally recognised qualifications, the critical-thinking and independent-learning skills that universities look for, and the confidence to make a success of their future.

Our completing students gain admission to leading universities worldwide, including in the UK, USA, Canada and South Africa.

  1. 1CheckpointYears 7–9Strong foundations in English, Mathematics and Science.
  2. 2IGCSEAges 15–17A globally recognised qualification, comparable to Grades 10 and 11.
  3. 3AS LevelAges 16+The first half of the A Level, comparable to the Grade 12 (matric) year.
  4. 4A LevelAges 17+Widely accepted for university entrance in South Africa and around the world.
  5. 5University & beyondYour futurePathways to universities in South Africa and abroad, and the skills to succeed there.

Where Cambridge qualifications can take you

Cambridge International AS and A Levels are recognised by universities in South Africa and around the world.

account_balance

South Africa

More than 26 public universities and 115 private higher education institutions in South Africa recognise Cambridge International AS and A Levels, and strong A Level results can earn up to a year’s credit towards some courses.

Admission to South African universities

United Kingdom

Cambridge International AS and A Levels are equivalent to the AS and A Levels taken in the UK, and are accepted for entrance to all UK universities.

Admission to UK universities

North America

Over 485 US universities accept Cambridge International AS and A Levels, including all Ivy League universities, and good grades can earn up to a year of course credit.

Admission to North American institutions

Worldwide

Students use Cambridge International AS and A Levels to gain places at leading universities in Europe, Australia, New Zealand, Asia and beyond.

Admission worldwide

Subject selection and choice

Choosing the right subjects matters. Our academic guidance helps each student plan their pathway and select IGCSE, AS and A Level subjects with their intended degree and career in mind.

At A Level, students normally take three to four subjects, although two may be sufficient depending on the university. You can check which institutions recognise Cambridge qualifications with the Cambridge recognition search.
