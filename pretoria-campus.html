Pretoria Campus

EMIS Number: 700400787

British International College Pretoria provides the same high academic standards and Cambridge International curriculum in a smaller, focused campus setting.

Located in the quiet suburb of Waverley, our Pretoria campus offers IGCSE, AS, and A Level programmes to students who value individual attention, small class sizes, and a highly supportive academic environment.

Like our Bryanston students, learners at BIC Pretoria regularly achieve exceptional Cambridge results and have received Top in the World and Top in South Africa awards.

Our experienced teachers work closely with each student to ensure they are fully prepared for academic success and university entrance.

Pretoria graduates have gone on to study at top institutions in South Africa and internationally.
