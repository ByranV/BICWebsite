Access Arrangements for Cambridge International Exams

Special adjustments that let candidates with specific needs show what they can do, without changing the standard of the exam.

Fair access for every candidate

Cambridge International strives to ensure that all candidates can demonstrate their abilities fairly. Access arrangements are adjustments to exams for candidates with specific needs, such as learning difficulties, disabilities, medical conditions or temporary injuries. They do not alter the standard of the exam; they enable candidates to perform to the best of their abilities.

Arrangements are tailored to each candidate and may include:

  • Extra time to complete the exam
  • Separate invigilation in a quieter room
  • Assistive technology, such as a word processor or specialised software
  • A reader or scribe to read questions aloud or record answers
  • Modified papers, such as larger text, braille or coloured overlays
  • Rest breaks to manage fatigue or medical conditions

Evidence and applications

To be eligible, candidates must provide evidence that clearly shows how their disability, illness or learning difficulty affects their ability to perform under standard exam conditions. The evidence must be:

  • Recent: medical reports or assessments dated within four years of the exam.
  • Verified by a suitable professional: for example, a medical professional, an educational, clinical or occupational psychologist, or an appropriately qualified specialist teacher.

Arrangements are granted on individual requirements, in line with Cambridge International’s guidelines.

info

Access arrangements apply to one exam session only. Candidates must reapply, and resubmit all supporting documents, for every exam series they register for.

A fee applies to each access arrangement service, covering the administration involved. Applications must be submitted well in advance of the exam date, so contact your exams officer or Student Advisor early to start the process.

Access arrangements are a vital part of our commitment to inclusivity and fairness, enabling all our candidates to achieve their full potential in Cambridge International exams.
