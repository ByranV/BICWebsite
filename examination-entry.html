BIDC students have the opportunity to enter for Cambridge Exams and our Distance College Exams (specific levels only).

As a registered student of BIDC, you are charged only for fees generated for your Cambridge Exam. Your registration includes any administration fees associated to writing at any of our Cambridge Exam Centres. We offer entries to Cambridge Exams in the May/June and the October/November Series (subject to availability).

BIDC Students who are not writing Cambridge Examinations may enter to write Distance College Exams (October only).

A consultation with your BIDC Student Advisor is recommended prior to entering for either Cambridge or BIDC Exams. You can then enter for Cambridge Exams, and generate a free quote for your chosen subjects, from your OSS Portal.

Entry Deadlines and Penalties

  • May/June Exam Series 31 January
  • October/November Exam Series 31 May

Exam entries submitted after these deadlines may still be accepted; however, they will incur an additional penalty fee. We strongly encourage timely submissions to avoid extra charges and ensure smooth processing.
