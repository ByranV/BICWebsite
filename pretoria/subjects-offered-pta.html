Courses Offered – Pretoria Campus

Cambridge IGCSE and AS & A Levels in a smaller, focused campus, preparing students for university and beyond.

From IGCSE to A Level, in a focused setting

BIC Pretoria follows the Cambridge International curriculum, developed by Cambridge International Education, part of the University of Cambridge. Taught in over 160 countries, it is valued for its academic rigour, international benchmarks and emphasis on independent thinking, preparing students for success at university and beyond.

At Pretoria we offer the IGCSE and AS & A Level stages of the Cambridge pathway, each building on the one before.

  3. 3University & beyondYour futureAdmission to universities in South Africa and abroad.

Starting earlier? Junior High (Years 7–9) is offered at our Bryanston campus, and students can move on to IGCSE at either campus.

Cambridge IGCSE

Ages 15–17

The International General Certificate of Secondary Education is a globally recognised, international school-leaving qualification, usually completed over two years. It prepares students for A Level study or equivalent programmes worldwide.

Students aged 15 to 17 who have completed Grade 8 or the Checkpoint stage choose between 7 and 9 subjects.

Subjects should include

  • English Language (First, Second or Foreign Language)
  • A second language
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Information Technology (IT)

trending_upGrowing independence: students take more responsibility for their learning as they prepare for their first Cambridge qualifications.

Cambridge International AS & A Level

Ages 16+

An internationally respected, academically rigorous qualification, and one of the most widely accepted for university admission around the world. In South Africa, A Level standards are considered equivalent to the first year of university study.

Open to students aged 16 and above who have completed IGCSE or the South African Matric. A Levels usually take two years, with the AS Level written at the end of the first year.

Typical programme

  • 3 to 4 subjects (2 may be enough for some universities)
  • AS Level after year 1
  • Full A Level after year 2
  • Admission to universities in South Africa and abroad

trending_upGrowing independence: students manage more of their own study time, preparing them for the independence of university.
